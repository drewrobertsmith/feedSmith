# Quality bar

What this app must never get wrong. `/expo-dev-loop:feature` plans, reviews and verifies every change against the items that apply to it; `/expo-dev-loop:pre-release` walks all of them. Built and revised with `/expo-dev-loop:quality-bar`. UI items Q29, Q30, Q32 and Q34 come from `docs/design-references/todoist.md`, the worked reference for structure and behaviour (D16).

**The app:** feedSmith is read by its one owner on an Android phone (a Pixel 10 on Android 17), one-handed, often in the evening or in transit. Sessions are studied reading, from a few minutes to half an hour.

## Release blockers

Q1, Q2, Q36, Q4, Q8, Q9, Q10, Q11, Q12, Q14, Q15, Q22, Q23, Q26, Q28, Q29, and Q32's "removing anything asks first". Any other failure, including a missed budget, becomes a ticket.

## Deferred

- **Offline reading** (T6): settled when the owner wants Articles readable without a connection.
- **Open original's use falls over time** (requirement 2, D39): settled by the owner's week-long tally of Open original uses (site and reason), which decides whether excerpt-only Feeds fetch the whole page.
- **Tablets, foldables and landscape:** settled when the owner wants a second layout.
- **Launch metrics with EAS Observe:** settled if the manual cold-start timing proves too laborious to run each release.
- **Wallpaper (Material You) colours** (D4): settled when the owner wants them; Uniwind themes keep the door open.
- **iOS** (T3): settled when the owner wants it.

## Not in the bar

- **VoiceOver and Dynamic Type** (stability baseline): v1 is Android only, so TalkBack and Android's font size replace them (Q15, Q16).
